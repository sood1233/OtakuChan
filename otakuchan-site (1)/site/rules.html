Rules

  1. No illegal content of any kind. This includes, without exception, child sexual abuse material — posts are logged and reported to the relevant authorities (NCMEC/law enforcement).
  2. No doxxing — do not post other people's private information (addresses, phone numbers, full legal names, etc).
  3. No spam, flooding, or advertising.
  4. No harassment, threats, or targeted abuse of individuals.
  5. Don't upload media you don't have the right to share; respect copyright.
  6. Anonymous posting is a privilege, not a shield — moderators can remove posts and block abusive senders.

Moderation

Every post can be reported with the "Report" link. Reports go to a moderation queue that isn't publicly readable. Moderators may remove any post or file for any reason, including rule violations.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Posting is fully anonymous — just type a name if you want one, otherwise you'll show up as "Anonymous".

What can I upload?

Images (JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP) and video (MP4, WebM), up to 5MB per file.

Are posts permanent?

Posts stay up unless removed by moderators for violating the rules, or reported and taken down.

DMCA / Copyright

If you believe content on this board infringes your copyright, contact the site operator with the URL of the material and a description of your claim, and it will be reviewed for removal.
