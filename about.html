About InteractInk

InteractInk is a community imageboard — a place to post, reply, and share images and video with real accounts instead of anonymous handles. Think of it as a mix of a classic imageboard's fast, casual posting style and the accountability of a normal social profile: everyone signs up with an email, username, and password, so conversations stay a little more grounded.

Who it's for

InteractInk is for people who want to post and talk in focused communities — around a topic, a fandom, a hobby, or just a shared sense of humor — without needing a big following or a polished profile first. Browsing is open to anyone; posting, replying, and liking just require a free account.

Why it exists

We wanted a place that keeps the best parts of imageboard culture — quick posting, image and video-first threads, communities built around specific interests — while still being a place people can be found and followed, build a small reputation, and have a real conversation instead of a wall of disconnected anonymous posts. That's InteractInk: real accounts, community-driven boards, and a straightforward, no-nonsense way to post.
